2. Core Subsystems

• Gate Attendance Engine (attendanceController.js): Decodes QR codes and uses unique registration indexing to reject duplicate gate scans with 409 Conflict.
• Certificate Engine (certificateController.js): Issues verifiable digital certificates to verified attendees with unique verification IDs.
• Role Guard Middleware (middleware/auth.js): Verifies token signatures and enforces role restrictions (Student, Organizer, Admin).
